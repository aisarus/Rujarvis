# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.
Contributors should communicate constructively, assume good faith while relying
on evidence, respect privacy, and focus criticism on work rather than people.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, doxxing, and deliberate disruption
are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer, [@aisarus](https://github.com/aisarus),
or through GitHub's *Report content* feature.
